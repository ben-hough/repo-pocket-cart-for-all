# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Shares PocketCartPlus's Keep Items upgrade with every player in the lobby (`AfterAnyoneConsumed` or `Always` mode).
- Host-controlled: the host's settings are published to the lobby, and guests do nothing unless the host runs the mod.
- HostAssist: the host pushes the shared level to guests who don't have the mod.
